# (1NZ-FE) Yaris Reverse Engineering
Reverse Engineering focused on the 1NZ-FE Platform for 2nd gen Yaris/Vitz.
My main focus is for this platform to be accesible in a plug and play fashion for the Rusefi Platform, although it could be done with other platforms if they can drive the ETB natively. I don't have a junker car or most parts to physically bench test, but I'll do my best to keep it updated as I make any progress on the project at all if any.

If you have anything that is useful to utilize the stock harness with a standalone ECU or pertinent info (like CANBUS chatter and labeled CANIDs) feel free to share it here, on any other repo, forum online or youtube video. Gatekeeping information just to possibly get a few bucks off from it rather than sharing it with everybody to access and analyze is honestly a waste of potential. These platforms are old, discontinued and not even popular at all. There honestly isn't a reason to share these things for the few people who like it other than abstaining from helping the platform stay alive.
